# Welcome to omajot 👋

omajot keeps your notes in **markdown**, on all your devices, and syncs them through **your own hub**.

- [x] Open omajot from the bar
- [x] Press `n` for a new note
- [ ] Scan the QR code with your phone
- [ ] Add omajot to your home screen

The first line of a note is its title. Words like #omajot become tags. Paste an image and it becomes an attachment.

Read more: [omajot on GitHub](https://github.com/renerocksai/omajot)
