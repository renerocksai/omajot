# Shakshuka

For two people, 25 minutes.

- [ ] 4 eggs
- [ ] 1 can of tomatoes
- [ ] 1 red pepper
- [ ] 1 onion, 2 cloves of garlic
- [ ] Cumin, paprika, chili
- [ ] Feta and parsley

1. Soften the onion and pepper in olive oil.
2. Add garlic and spices, then the tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Make four hollows and crack in the eggs.
4. Cover and cook for 6–8 minutes. Add feta and parsley.
